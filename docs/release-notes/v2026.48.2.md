# open-3d-viewer 2026.48.2

Open BIM Suite, with the Viewer, the BIM Validator, Docs, GIC, Library, Pointcloud (alpha),
Structural Analysis (alpha) and the Modeller (alpha) behind the switch at the top right of the
window. On top of [2026.48.1](v2026.48.1.md):

## Modeller

- One **Frame** button for a door or a window frame; the Model tab has a group **Objects** with
  Frame and Stair, and the Roof sits with the Wall and the Slab in the group **Plane**.
- Grid lines: select all at once (Ctrl+A), change their length, and lock them (pin / unpin) so
  they are not changed by accident. Picked and locked grid lines are marked in the plan views too.
- A kernel and Modeller that stay fast on a large model, materials and Dutch hatching, doors in plan.

## Models and files

- Models are aligned by two pairs of points, replaced by a new revision in place, and keep their
  turn in the project.
- Readers no longer bring the program down on a damaged file: the reading is guarded and the
  readers are tested against damaged input.
- `place_model`: the models' places through the control port and MCP.

## BIM Validator

- Views and classification made of rules, kept in the rule set; a clash matrix by class, with
  tolerance, weight and agreement per pair.
- What is clicked in a takeoff or an issue is isolated; dimension labels keep clear of one another.
- An issue's dimensions go with it, a dropped file is added, and Ctrl+S saves.

## Viewer and interface

- W, A, S and D move through the model in every 3D view, set in the settings.
- Zooming far in no longer cuts the model away, and a setting cuts at the eye.
- A frame of the interface costs 3 ms on a large model, not 42.
- The settings dialog keeps one size on every tab.

## Drawings and sheets

- Sheets are exported as a series at once: one PDF to print, or a PDF, DWG, DXF or SVG per sheet.
- Element drawings: no number of a part stands through another.
