# open-3d-viewer 2026.48.1

Open BIM Suite, with the Viewer, the BIM Validator, Docs, GIC, Library, Pointcloud (alpha),
Structural Analysis (alpha) and the Modeller (alpha) behind the switch at the top right of the
window. On top of [2026.47.5](v2026.47.5.md):

## An installer of its own for the BIM Validator

- Next to the suite's installer, Windows gets `open-bim-validator_2026.48.1_x64-setup.exe`: the
  same program, installed in a folder of its own, whose shortcut **Open BIM Validator** starts it
  on the BIM Validator (the Collaborator before). It replaces the separate BIM Validator app of
  2026.45, which is no longer developed.

## BIM Validator

- A clash is looked at in section, and its issue gets a picture of it.
- A model is moved by two points.

## Viewer and navigation

- Orbit, zoom and pan go about the point under the cursor.
- DGN files open through the independent `microstation-dll` library.
- Narrow ribbons fold their groups; sheets, drafts and paper stay inside their pane when zoomed.
- Soundings are shown as soundings.

## Drawings and sheets

- Drawings draw with the line styles of their kind.
- The title block is typed into on the sheet itself.
- A booklet: every sheet of the document in one tab, and as one PDF.
- Structural drawings: beam labels, a pile plan with its schedule, element drawings and fin plates.

## Modeller (alpha)

- Align (AL), level marks, copying grids, and the split of the work.
- Systems: slab floors with cores, a division to choose, ceilings; sand-lime elements with a
  lintel over every opening.
- Building services: ducts, pipes and cable trays, with bends and tees as connections.
- Steel splices and base plates, reinforcement with a bar schedule, precast plank marks.
- Exact solids: round columns, tubes and bowed slab edges count with their true volume, and the
  model goes out as STEP (**File → Export STEP…**).

## Library

- Objects, connection types, service types and hatch standards; the generic 2D components of
  Project Ocondat, each on its layer, with their drawings.
- Materials: their physics with a source and a date, a price, NL-SfB codes, strength classes
  (concrete, steel, reinforcement, bolts, prestressing steel, aluminium, mortar) and a concrete's
  exposure classes.
- What a Dutch delivery asks (BIM basis ILS) of build-ups, linear types, system types, doors,
  windows, stairs and railings, and the stock of libraries for it.
- Levels of detail 200, 350 and 500, and the level a project's drawings show.
- Editors for view templates and the rules of drawing sets; line patterns, line weights at a
  scale and line styles; the house style names its hatch standard and reads and saves a
  Blueprint brand file.
- A build-up's price per square metre from its materials, and costs in Open Calc Studio's terms.

## For developers and agents

- `O3V_INSTANCE` starts a viewer of its own for development, with a registered MCP server.
- Remote `navigate` zooms and pans an open sheet.
- IFCX is saved and reopened through the merged `ifcx-core`.
